## Narration script (spoken)

1. Welcome to the wonderful world of Connie.

2. In this short video, we'll look at a brand-new feature that came highly requested —
   straight from staff agents like you. It's called Task Alerts.

3. We know Connie is just one of the many hats you wear in a day. You're logged in and
   ready to help — but you're also juggling everything else on your plate.

4. The worry is always the same: "What if a task comes in and I miss it because I'm
   heads-down on something else?"

5. Task Alerts puts that worry to rest. As long as you're logged in to Connie, a friendly
   notification pops up the moment a new task arrives — even when Connie isn't the window
   you're looking at.

6. Heads-down in your email, a spreadsheet, or another browser tab? Connie gives you a
   gentle tap on the shoulder.

7. When the alert appears, just click it — and Connie brings you right to the task,
   ready to go.

8. And you're in control. The very first time, your browser will ask if Connie can show
   you notifications. Click Allow, and you're all set.

9. Want to adjust it later? It all lives in your Chrome settings. Click the icon to the
   left of the web address, open Site settings, and you can turn notifications on or off
   for Connie any time you like.

10. So stay focused on everything else you do — and let Connie keep an eye on your tasks
    for you.

11. Just like that, nothing falls through the cracks.

12. Thanks for using Connie. We can't wait to see all the amazing things you do with it.
