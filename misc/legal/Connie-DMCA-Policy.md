## Required Information for DMCA Notice

Please include the following information in your Notice:

### Contact Information
- **Full name**
- **Email address**
- **Mailing address** (optional)
- **Phone number** (optional)

### Copyright Ownership Information
- **Name of copyright owner**
- **Your relationship to the copyright owner** (e.g., owner, authorized agent)

### Infringement Details
- **The copyrighted work that you are claiming has been infringed** (identify the work with a URL and/or a description)
- **The material that you are claiming is infringing or is the subject of infringing activity** and sufficient information for Connie to locate the infringing material or discover the infringing activity (such as URLs, examples, or a description)

### Legal Statement
A statement that:
- You have a **good faith belief** that the use of the material in the manner described in your notice is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
- The information in this notice is **accurate**
- **Under penalty of perjury**, you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner

### Signature
- Your physical or electronic signature
- Date of the notice

## Our Response to DMCA Notices

Upon receipt of a complete DMCA Notice, Connie will take whatever action it deems appropriate, in its sole discretion, including:
- Removal or disabling of the content at issue from our products or services
- Notification to the user who posted the allegedly infringing content
- Termination of repeat infringers' accounts

## Disclosure of Reporter Information

We may provide your contact information, including:
- Your name and email address
- The name of the copyright owner
- The contents of your DMCA Notice

to the party using the material you are reporting. We may also contact you if we have questions about your Notice.

## Counter-Notification Process

If you believe that material you posted was wrongfully removed or disabled, you may send a counter-notification to our DMCA Agent containing:

### Required Counter-Notice Information
- Your name, address, telephone number, and email address
- Identification of the material that was removed and its location before removal
- A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification
- A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or [JURISDICTION] if outside the United States)
- A statement that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification
- Your physical or electronic signature

## Repeat Infringer Policy

Connie maintains a policy to terminate, in appropriate circumstances, users who are repeat infringers of intellectual property rights.

## False Claims Warning

Please be aware that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing, or that material or activity was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, may be subject to liability.
