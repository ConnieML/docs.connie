# Connie Data Processing Agreement

**Last Updated:** [DATE]  
**Effective Date:** [DATE]

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Agreement between ConnieML, Inc. ("Connie," "we," "us," or "our") and the Customer identified in the Agreement ("Customer," "you," or "your") for the provision of Connie Services.

## 1. Definitions

**"Agreement"** means the Connie Terms of Service or other written agreement between Connie and Customer for the provision of Services.

**"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data.

**"Data Protection Laws"** means all applicable legislation protecting the fundamental rights and freedoms of persons and their right to privacy with regard to Processing Personal Data, including GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable laws.

**"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable person to whom Personal Data relates.

**"GDPR"** means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation).

**"Personal Data"** means any information relating to a Data Subject that is Processed by Connie on behalf of Customer.

**"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.

**"Processor"** means the entity that Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

**"Security Incident"** means any unauthorized or unlawful breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of or access to Personal Data.

**"Services"** means the Connie communication platform services provided to Customer.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by Connie to Process Personal Data.

## 2. Data Processing

### 2.1 Roles of the Parties
The parties acknowledge that with regard to Processing of Personal Data:
- Customer is the Controller
- Connie is the Processor
- Connie will Process Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions

### 2.2 Customer Instructions
Customer instructs Connie to Process Personal Data:
- To provide the Services in accordance with the Agreement
- As initiated by End Users in their use of the Services
- To comply with other documented reasonable instructions provided by Customer
- As required by applicable law

### 2.3 Details of Processing

**Subject Matter:** Processing of Personal Data in connection with Connie's communication platform services

**Duration:** The term of the Agreement plus any retention period required by law

**Nature and Purpose:** To provide communication services including voice, fax, email, and web chat capabilities for nonprofit and community-based organizations

**Types of Personal Data:**
- Contact information (names, email addresses, phone numbers)
- Communication content (messages, call recordings, voicemails)
- Communication metadata (timestamps, duration, routing information)
- Account and authentication data
- Usage and analytics data

**Categories of Data Subjects:**
- Customer's employees and staff
- Customer's clients and beneficiaries
- Customer's vendors and partners
- Other individuals who communicate with Customer

## 3. Connie's Obligations

### 3.1 Compliance
Connie shall:
- Process Personal Data in accordance with Customer's instructions
- Ensure that persons authorized to Process Personal Data have committed to confidentiality
- Implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures
- Not engage Sub-processors without Customer authorization
- Assist Customer in responding to Data Subject requests
- Assist Customer with security, breach notifications, and compliance obligations
- Delete or return Personal Data upon termination
- Make available information necessary to demonstrate compliance

### 3.2 Security Measures
Connie implements appropriate technical and organizational measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication mechanisms
- Regular security assessments and audits
- Employee training on data protection
- Incident response procedures
- Business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- Physical security controls for data centers

### 3.3 Data Subject Rights
Connie shall assist Customer in fulfilling obligations to respond to Data Subject requests including:
- Access to Personal Data
- Rectification or erasure of Personal Data
- Restriction of Processing
- Data portability
- Objection to Processing

## 5. Security Incidents

### 5.1 Incident Notification
Connie shall notify Customer without undue delay and within 72 hours after becoming aware of a Security Incident affecting Customer's Personal Data.

### 5.2 Incident Information
Notifications shall include:
- Nature of the Security Incident
- Categories and approximate number of affected Data Subjects
- Categories and approximate number of affected Personal Data records
- Likely consequences of the Security Incident
- Measures taken or proposed to address the Security Incident

### 5.3 Incident Response
Connie shall:
- Investigate the Security Incident
- Take reasonable steps to mitigate harmful effects
- Document all Security Incidents and actions taken
- Cooperate with Customer's investigation

## 6. International Transfers

### 6.1 Transfer Mechanisms
Where Processing involves transfers of Personal Data outside the EEA, Connie shall ensure appropriate safeguards through:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (incorporated by reference)
- Adequacy decisions
- Other legally approved transfer mechanisms

### 6.2 Standard Contractual Clauses
The parties agree that the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission shall apply to transfers of Personal Data from the EEA to countries without an adequacy decision.

## 7. Audits

### 7.1 Audit Rights
Customer may audit Connie's compliance with this DPA:
- Up to once per year
- Upon reasonable notice (at least 30 days)
- During regular business hours
- Subject to confidentiality obligations
- At Customer's expense

### 7.2 Third-Party Certifications
Connie may provide third-party certifications and audit reports in lieu of Customer audits, including:
- SOC 2 Type II reports
- ISO certifications
- Other industry-standard compliance reports

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

### 8.1 Retention Period
Connie will retain Personal Data for the duration of the Agreement and as required for:
- Legal obligations
- Dispute resolution
- Enforcement of agreements
- Legitimate business purposes

### 8.2 Deletion
Upon termination of the Agreement, Connie shall:
- Delete all Personal Data within 90 days
- Provide certification of deletion upon request
- Retain only data required by law

## 9. Liability

### 9.1 Liability Cap
Each party's liability under this DPA shall be subject to the limitations of liability in the Agreement.

### 9.2 Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify the other against damages arising from its breach of this DPA or Data Protection Laws.

## 10. CCPA Provisions

For Personal Data subject to CCPA:
- Connie is a "Service Provider" as defined by CCPA
- Connie shall not sell Personal Data
- Connie shall not retain, use, or disclose Personal Data except as necessary for the Services
- Connie certifies that it understands and will comply with CCPA restrictions

### 12.3 Termination
