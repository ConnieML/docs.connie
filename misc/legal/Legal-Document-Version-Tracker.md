## Status Definitions

### Document Status:
- **Draft** - Initial version, needs review
- **In Review** - Legal team reviewing
- **Needs Revision** - Changes requested
- **Final** - Approved and ready
- **Published** - Live on website

### Legal Review Status:
- **Pending Review** - Waiting for legal review
- **Under Review** - Legal team actively reviewing  
- **Revision Requested** - Changes needed
- **Approved** - Legal sign-off complete
- **Published** - Live and in effect

## Workflow Process

### Phase 1: Initial Review
1. Upload DOCX files to Google Drive folder
2. Convert to Google Docs (auto-conversion)
3. Set all reviewers to "Suggesting" mode
4. Update tracker with Google Docs links
5. Notify legal team

### Phase 2: Legal Review
1. Legal team reviews in "Suggesting" mode
2. Add comments for discussion points
3. Update tracker with review status
4. CEO reviews suggestions and accepts/rejects
5. Iterate until approved

### Phase 3: Final Approval
1. Accept all approved suggestions
2. Export clean version back to DOCX
3. Convert to MD and commit to repository
4. Update tracker to "Published" status
5. Archive review versions
