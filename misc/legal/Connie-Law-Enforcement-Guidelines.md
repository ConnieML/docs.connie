## Connie's Commitment to User Privacy

Connie is committed to protecting the privacy of its users while also complying with applicable laws. We carefully review each law enforcement request to ensure it satisfies applicable legal requirements.

## Types of Customer Information

Connie may maintain the following types of customer information:

### Basic Account Information
- Account holder name
- Account creation date
- Email address
- Billing information (if applicable)
- IP address logs

### Usage Information
- Call Detail Records (CDRs)
- Message logs (metadata only)
- API usage logs
- Service configuration data

### Communications Content
- Stored voicemails
- Recorded calls (if recording is enabled)
- Message content (where stored)
- Fax content

## Legal Process Requirements

### Subpoena (Administrative, Grand Jury, or Trial)
**Information Available:**
- Basic account information
- Account activity logs
- IP address information

**Not Available via Subpoena:**
- Communications content
- Real-time information
- Location information beyond IP address

### Court Order (18 U.S.C. § 2703(d))
**Information Available:**
- Everything available via subpoena
- Extended account records
- Session times and durations
- Historical IP addresses

### Search Warrant
**Information Available:**
- All available account information
- Communications content
- Stored data
- Any information in Connie's possession

### Wiretap Order (Title III)
Required for real-time interception of communications content.

### Pen Register/Trap and Trace Order
Required for real-time, prospective non-content information.

## Emergency Requests

Connie evaluates emergency requests on a case-by-case basis. Emergency requests must:

1. Come from a law enforcement agency
2. Involve imminent danger of:
   - Death or serious physical injury to a person
   - Child exploitation or abduction
3. Require immediate disclosure to prevent harm

## User Notification

Connie's policy is to notify users of law enforcement requests for their information unless:
- Prohibited by law or court order
- Emergency circumstances exist
- Notice would jeopardize investigation
- Valid non-disclosure order is provided

## Data Preservation Requests

Connie will preserve account information for 90 days upon receipt of a valid preservation request. Extensions may be granted for an additional 90 days upon request.

**Preservation requests must include:**
- Account identifiers
- Types of data to preserve
- Date range
- Case information
- Expected date of formal legal process

## International Requests

Connie is headquartered in the United States. International law enforcement requests should be served via:
- Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty (MLAT)
- Letters Rogatory
- Other applicable international agreements

Direct requests from international law enforcement may be considered for emergency situations only.

## Cost Reimbursement

Connie may seek reimbursement for costs associated with responding to law enforcement requests as permitted by law, including:
- Research time
- Data preparation
- Technical services
- Legal review

## Testimony

Connie does not provide expert testimony. Witness testimony may be available for:
- Authentication of records
- Chain of custody
- Business record foundation

Testimony requests require separate subpoena and reasonable advance notice.

## Records Authentication

Connie can provide:
- Business records affidavit
- Certificate of authenticity
- Custodian of records declaration
