## How Our Support System Works

1. **Start with L1 (Documentation)** - Most questions can be answered quickly through our comprehensive knowledge base
2. **Connect with L2 (Community)** - Get insights from experienced users and share your own solutions  
3. **Escalate to L3 (Tickets)** - Submit formal requests for technical issues that need detailed investigation
4. **Access L4 (Live Chat)** - Get immediate help for critical issues affecting your operations
5. **Engage L5 (Professional Services)** - Partner with experts for strategic implementations and custom solutions

---

*All support tiers connect seamlessly through our support portal at [connie.plus/support](https://connie.plus/support)*
