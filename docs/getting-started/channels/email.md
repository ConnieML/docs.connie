# Email Channel

Manage email communications efficiently with ConnieRTC's integrated email channel capabilities.

## Overview

The Email channel centralizes all email communications into your unified contact center, ensuring no message goes unanswered and maintaining consistent response quality.

## Getting Started

1. **Connect Email Account** - Link your email service
2. **Configure Routing** - Set up distribution rules
3. **Create Templates** - Build response templates library
4. **Set SLA Rules** - Define response time targets
5. **Train Your Team** - Ensure consistent email handling

## Best Practices

- Respond to emails within 24 hours
- Use templates for consistency
- Include signature with contact options
- Track response times and quality
- Regular template updates based on common questions

## Reporting & Analytics

- Average response time
- First contact resolution rate
- Email volume trends
- Agent productivity metrics
- Customer satisfaction scores
