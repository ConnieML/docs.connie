# Remove or Deactivate an Agent

> **Status:** Drafting. Authoritative content arrives when the internal runbook migration (CON-TBD) surfaces source material. Avoid following this page as a procedure until it is fully published.

When an agent leaves your organization, takes extended leave, or changes role, you can remove or deactivate their Connie account. Deactivation is reversible; removal is permanent.

## What this page will cover

- The difference between **deactivation** (temporary, account preserved) and **removal** (permanent, history retained but account closed)
- Pre-offboarding checklist (reassign in-flight tasks, transfer ownership of saved templates, etc.)
- Step-by-step deactivation flow
- Step-by-step removal flow
- What the system does automatically (sign out active sessions, stop routing new work, preserve historical reports)
- Audit-trail expectations for funder reporting and compliance

## In the meantime

To deactivate or remove an agent today, contact your Connie deployment team or open a support ticket with:

1. The agent's Connie email
2. Deactivation or removal
3. Effective date and time
4. Whether any of their in-flight tasks need to be reassigned (and to whom)
