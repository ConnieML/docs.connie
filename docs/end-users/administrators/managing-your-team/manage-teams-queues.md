# Manage Teams & Queues

> **Status:** Drafting. Authoritative content arrives when the internal runbook migration (CON-TBD) surfaces source material. Avoid following this page as a procedure until it is fully published.

Teams group agents for supervisor oversight and reporting. Queues route work to the right pool of agents based on skills, channel, and priority.

## What this page will cover

- How teams and queues relate (one agent can be on multiple teams; queues match by skill, not team)
- Creating or renaming a team
- Adding or removing an agent from a team
- Queue configuration — priority, overflow, after-hours behavior
- Common patterns (one team per program, one queue per channel × language, etc.)

## In the meantime

To change team membership or queue configuration today, contact your Connie deployment team or open a support ticket with:

1. The team or queue name
2. The change requested
3. Effective date if not immediate
