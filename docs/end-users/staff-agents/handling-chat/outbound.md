# Starting New Conversations

You can start a new SMS or chat conversation with a client directly from Connie.

## Sending a New SMS

1. Click **"+ New task"** or the compose button in the Task Manager area
2. Select **SMS** as the channel
3. Enter the client's **phone number**
4. Type your message
5. Click **"Send"**

The conversation appears in your Task Queue as an active task. If the client replies, their response shows up in the same conversation thread.

## Starting a Webchat or Messaging Conversation

Webchat and messaging app conversations (WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger) are typically **initiated by the client** — they reach out to your organization through a chat widget on your website or through their messaging app.

However, depending on your organization's setup, you may be able to initiate outbound messages on some channels. Check with your supervisor about what's available.

## Completing an Outbound Message Task

After your conversation is done:

1. Click **"End Chat"** to close the conversation
2. Select a **Disposition** (closing code)
3. Add **notes** about the conversation
4. Click **"Complete"**

## Quick Reference

| Action | How to Do It |
|--------|-------------|
| Send a new SMS | + New task → SMS → Enter number → Type message → Send |
| End conversation | End Chat → Select disposition → Add notes → Complete |
