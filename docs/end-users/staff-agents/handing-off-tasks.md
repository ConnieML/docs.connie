# Handing Off & Referring Out Tasks

:::tip New: one Send button for every task
Hand off and Refer out now live on the **Send** button at the top of every task. For the full picture, including **Transfer**, see **[Connie Task Sender](/end-users/staff-agents/task-sender)**.
:::

:::info Tasks stay for 96 hours
A task that arrives while you're closed will **still be waiting for four full days** — long enough to survive a weekend, and a holiday weekend. Close Friday at 5:30 PM, an email lands at 5:31 PM, and it's still there when someone starts Monday morning.

Live phone calls and web chats are the exception; their window is much shorter, because someone is on the line right now.

Need a different window for a program or channel? Custom settings are available — contact your Connie account representative or the [Connie Care Team](/get-support/overview). See **[How Long a Task Stays in Your Queue](/end-users/staff-agents/handling-tasks/how-long-tasks-last)**.
:::

Sometimes a task belongs with someone who **doesn't use Connie**. You can email it to them, with everything attached, in a couple of clicks.

| Choice | Who gets it | Does Connie keep track? |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand off** | A coworker at your organization who does **not** use Connie (for example, someone in the front office). | **No.** It leaves Connie and closes. |
| **Refer out** | Someone at **another organization** (a clinic, a partner agency, a caseworker). | **No.** It leaves Connie and closes. |

Want to keep it in Connie instead? Use **[Transfer](/end-users/staff-agents/transferring-tasks)**.

## 🔧 What You Need to Know

- Hand off and Refer out work on **voicemail, fax, web form and email** tasks.
- They do **not** work on a phone call, web chat or text. A real person is waiting with you, and you can't email someone who is on the phone with you. For a live person, use **Transfer** (or connect a caller to an outside number using the **External** tab or the dial pad).
- Everything goes with it: the recording and transcript for a voicemail, the document for a fax or form, or the full email with its attachments.
- **The task closes in Connie.** Connie will not remind anyone or follow up.

## 📤 How to Hand off or Refer out

1. Press **Send**, then **Hand off** (coworker without Connie) or **Refer out** (another organization).
2. Pick the person from the list.
3. Press **Send**. Connie asks you to check: *"This closes the task in Connie. Nobody here will be reminded about it."* Press **Send** again to confirm.
4. Pick a disposition in wrap-up and press **Complete**.

## 📇 Who Shows Up in the List?

People in your contacts who have an **email address** saved.

- **Hand off** shows people whose email is at **your own organization**.
- **Refer out** shows everyone else.
- Your **My Contacts** are private: only you see them and only you can send to them.
- **Shared Contacts** are your organization's address book; an admin or supervisor looks after them — see [Shared Contacts & External Referrals](/end-users/administrators/shared-contacts-and-referrals).

## ⏸️ Holding a Voicemail (Don't Send It Anywhere)

If you just need to **wait** — a callback, a doctor's office, more research — you don't have to send it on. On a voicemail, use **Retry Later** to set it aside; it comes back to be worked later. See [Parking Tasks](/end-users/staff-agents/parking-tasks).

## 🔍 What Happens Next

- The person receives an email with everything attached.
- **If they reply**, the reply comes back into Connie as a **new task**.
- The task drops off your list, and the move is recorded, so reports show what was handed off or referred out.

## ⚠️ Common Questions

**"The person I want isn't in the list."**
They don't have an email address saved. Ask your admin or supervisor to add one.

**"I don't see Hand off or Refer out on the Send menu."**
You're probably on a phone call, web chat or text — those can only be transferred. If you're on a voicemail, fax, form or email and still don't see them, ask your supervisor. The Task Sender is switched on for each organization by the Connie team.

**"Did they actually get it?"**
The email is sent right away. If someone says it didn't arrive, ask them to check their spam folder, then let your supervisor know.

## 🚀 Quick Reference

| I want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Keep it in Connie** (coworker or department) | Send → **Transfer** |
| **Email it to a coworker who doesn't use Connie** | Send → **Hand off** → pick → Send → Send again |
| **Email it to another organization** | Send → **Refer out** → pick → Send → Send again |
| **Hold a voicemail to work later** | **Retry Later** on the voicemail |

Need help? Ask your supervisor or [contact Connie Support](/get-support/overview).
