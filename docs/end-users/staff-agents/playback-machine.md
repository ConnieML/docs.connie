# Finding Past Voicemails, Faxes and Forms

Sometimes you need something that came in days ago. A voicemail you want to hear again. A fax from a doctor's office. A form a client signed last week.

The **Playback Machine** keeps these in one place. You can play a voicemail or open a fax or form, any time, without needing the original task.

:::tip What's new
- **Everything in one place.** Voicemails, faxes and forms in one list, newest first.
- **Forms are new.** Signed web forms now sit right beside your voicemails and faxes.
- **Private by design.** Only staff signed in to ThreadConnect can see your messages.
- **Nothing to install.** It's already in your ThreadConnect panel.
:::

---

## Where to find it

The Playback Machine lives inside **ThreadConnect**. You must be signed in to ThreadConnect to use it.

1. Make sure you are **not** working on a task. Finish or close the task you have open.
2. Look at the **side panel**. With no task open, it shows the ThreadConnect home page.
3. Click **Admin Tools & Data**.
4. Click **Data & Reporting**.
5. Find the card called **Playback Machine**.

The card has four buttons:

| Button | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Load Voicemails** | Voicemails left for your team |
| **Load Faxes** | Faxes sent and received |
| **Load Forms** | Signed web forms |
| **All Media** | Everything together in one list, with filters |

---

## What each list shows

Every list shows the **last 30 days**, newest first. There is no limit on how many items it shows: if it came in during the last 30 days, it is in the list.

### Voicemails

Messages callers left for your team.

- You see the date, who called, and how long the message is.
- The list shows **voicemails only**. Recorded phone calls are not listed here.

### Faxes

Faxes your team sent or received.

- You see the date, the other fax number, whether it came in or went out, and the number of pages.

### Forms

Web forms that clients filled out and signed (Adobe forms).

- You see the date, the **name of the form**, and **who signed it**.
- If ThreadConnect can't tell who signed, you'll see a dash (**—**) instead of a name.

### All Media

One list with voicemails, faxes and forms mixed together, newest first.

Use the buttons at the top to narrow it down:

- **All** shows everything.
- **Voicemail**, **Fax** or **Form** shows just that kind. Click it again to show everything.
- **Refresh** checks for anything new.

Each button shows a count, so you can see at a glance how many of each there are.

---

## How to use it

### Play a voicemail

1. Find the voicemail in the list.
2. Click **Play**.
3. A small audio player appears. Use it to pause, replay or skip ahead.

Use headphones if you're in a shared space. These messages are private.

### Open a fax or form

1. Find the fax or form in the list.
2. Click **Open PDF** (on the Faxes page, the button says **View PDF**).
3. The document opens on the page, below the buttons.
4. Click **Close** when you're done.

For privacy, each file opens for a short time only. If a voicemail or document stops loading after a few minutes, click **Play** or **Open PDF** again.

---

## If you see "Sign in from ThreadConnect"

This means the page was opened **outside ThreadConnect**, for example in a separate browser tab, from a bookmark, or from a link someone sent you.

**What to do:** go back to ThreadConnect and open the Playback Machine from the side panel, as shown above.

For your clients' privacy, voicemails, faxes and forms only show to a person who is **signed in to ThreadConnect** for your organization. That is on purpose.

### If you see "Your sign-in expired"

Reload the ThreadConnect panel, then open the Playback Machine again. If that does not help, sign out of ThreadConnect and sign back in.

---

## There is no public link

The Playback Machine does not have a link you can share or bookmark. Opened anywhere other than your ThreadConnect panel, it shows nothing.

- **Old bookmarks no longer work.** If you saved a bookmark to an older voicemail or fax page, including any link with **twil.io** in it, delete it.
- **Don't send links to colleagues.** Tell them to open the Playback Machine from their own ThreadConnect panel.
- **Voicemail emails:** the link inside a voicemail notification email asks for a login you don't have. Play the attached audio file instead, or find the voicemail in the Playback Machine.

---

## Good to know

- **Only your organization.** You only ever see your own organization's voicemails, faxes and forms.
- **30 days back.** The lists only go back 30 days. If you need something older, talk to your supervisor.
- **It's for looking back.** Playing or opening a file here does not delete it or close any task.
